01 · GROUP A · STAR POWER

CAN ANYONE STOP CANADA'S OPENING RUN?

Canada begins against Switzerland, Slovakia and Great Britain. That means three very different roster builds in about 70 minutes. I Parksy x I76I ($8M) and ProdsznK ($7M) headline Canada's heavy commitment, but the bigger question is who provides support around the two prized acquisitions. Our editorial call: a 3–0 start is possible, but Japan could still finish level on night one.

02 · GROUP B · STATEMENT GAME

GERMANY VS SWEDEN.

At 8:35 PM, the league's highest-bidding spender meets a more balanced contender. Germany invested $22M in its winning bids, with MythicI9I leading the way at $7M. Sweden's Blehdes–vSkoon pair cost $10.5M combined. We lean Germany narrowly, but this game should give us a better clue about who can survive tight 3s rotations.
