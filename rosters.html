HOW THE CAP WORKS

Original draft value stays attached

A player's salary is based on where they were drafted, not the team they currently play for.

TRADE EXAMPLE Round 1 player ($7M) ↔ Round 2 player ($6M)

The team receiving the $7M player uses $1M more cap. The team receiving the $6M player gains $1M cap space.

League salary scale: Round 1 $7M, Round 2 $6M, Round 3 $5M, Round 4 $4M, Round 5 $3M, Round 6 $2M, and Round 7 or later $1M.
